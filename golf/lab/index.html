Video reference

Your face-on video of Brittany, for her arms, her hands and the space between them and her body. Club

The lead fist holds the grip end. Fixed in the lead hand (the held-weapon rule): the club turns with her lead hand like a held weapon; the grip is two angles in that hand (from the thumb line toward the fingers, and out of the palm), solved once at address so the clubhead sole rests on the ground with the hands the set distance ahead of the head (face-on), then the face is squared. The same grip rides in the reference's lead hand. The other two lines are kept for comparison: through the reference skeleton's two hands as captured, or through her own two fists.

Hands

The grip, both hands: the handle runs diagonally across the fingers (lead: from under the heel pad to the middle of the index finger), the thumbs lie down the shaft, the back of the lead hand and the trail palm face the target. Each grip is two angles in that hand. The trail fist sits the set distance down the grip; her trail arm reaches it in the source's elbow plane. The lead wrist keeps the mocap's motion with a correction set at address, top, impact and finish (solved by the button above: clubhead on the ground with the hands ahead at address and impact, shaft level and pointing at the target at the top, behind her head at the finish).
