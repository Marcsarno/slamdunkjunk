WHAT THESE FILES ARE
These are concept images and texture/decal studies, not newly modeled or production-ready 3D assets. No Blender files, meshes, new models, or older asset ZIPs are included. Multiple options may appear together on one concept sheet; they have not been split into separate assets. Original PNGs are preserved without resizing or pixel edits.

IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Landmark front/side views are illustrative concepts, not exact orthographic drawings or dimensionally matched modeling blueprints.
- Spiderwebs are native 1254 x 1254 RGBA images with transparency, not 2048 x 2048. Preserve their alpha channel during import.
- Spiderweb A and C are too circular for the intended Spiderwood direction. B is the closest starting point, but still needs a broader, more irregular redesign. These are existing options, not that completed redesign.
- Keep the white web decal separate from the purple ground. Tint the web lavender in-game rather than treating purple as part of its background.
- Hedge and ravine texture edges have visible repetition seams. These are not production-seamless textures; they need edge cleanup before continuous tiling. Ravine shading is painterly rather than physically neutral albedo.
- Course layouts communicate visual direction and obstacle ideas, not surveyed dimensions, final collision geometry, or verified playable construction plans.

Duplicate generation intermediates, prompt files, upload helpers, source-reference photos, and QA composites are omitted for a clean art-only collection.
