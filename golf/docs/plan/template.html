Graveyard Shift: Midnight Golf · Course plan

The Midnight Nine

Nine holes, each asking a different question. No two in a row bend the same way, use the same hazard, or end on the same kind of green. Every hole has a safe way round and a brave way through, and the cart path ties it together with a bridge at every crossing.

What makes it varied

  • A new question each holeCarry it, cut it, thread it, choose a road, or go for it in two. Each hole has one signature decision.
  • Bends both waysLeft, straight, S, right, right, straight, left, S, S. The eye never settles into one shot shape.
  • Lengths that swingFrom a 135-yard wedge to a 500-yard finale, with a drivable 270-yard par 4 in the middle.
  • Different greensAn island, two tiers, a creek shelf, a peninsula, a bunker ring, a punchbowl.
  • Brave line, safe lineThe game starts aimed safe (the dashed line). The gold line is the risk you can choose.
  • Hazards that changePond, island, graveyard split, ravine, diagonal cape, marsh, hedge maze and moat, ridge, stream.

Fun on top of the layout

Ideas for later, once the shapes are built. Each one is small and fits a hole that's already designed for it.

  • Tombstone kicks. Graveyards already bounce the ball. On hole 3 a lucky kick off a stone can pop it back onto the fairway.
  • Bridge bounces. A ball that lands on a bridge rattles across the planks and runs on instead of splashing.
  • The drifting wisps. On hole 6 two wisps float across the line. A ball that clips one gets a little gust.
  • The Horseman. On hole 8 he gallops along the ridge after a good drive on the high road.
  • The homecoming. Hole the last putt and the village lanterns light up one by one round the green.

What building it takes

Holes 1 and 2 stay as they are. The other seven are redesigns, and each layout on this page is already written in the course builder's own format, so building one is a rebuild in Blender plus a test round.

New landmarks I'd model in Blender, like the jack-o'-lantern: the windmill (hole 5), the castle drawbridge (hole 7), the covered bridge (hole 8) and the village square (hole 9). Spider webs (hole 4) and hedge-maze blocks (hole 7) would look best as painted art if you can make them.
